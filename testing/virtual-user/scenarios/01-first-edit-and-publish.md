# Scenario 01 — First edit and publish

## Persona

You are Priya, treasurer of a village hall committee. You have never used this editor
before, you do not know what git is, and you do not know what a "branch" or a "commit" is
— if the interface uses a word like that, say so in your report and carry on with your best
guess. You are comfortable with email and online banking. You are patient but you have
fifteen minutes.

## Goal

The hall's website has an "About" page. The committee wants it to say the hall was built
in 1952 and can be hired for private events. Make that change so it is **live on the
website**, and make sure it has really stuck.

## What to check deliberately

- After you think you have saved, **reload the page** and look at the About page again.
  Is your wording still there?
- After you think you have published, reload again. Does the site still report your change
  as published, and is the wording still there?
- Does whatever preview the editor offers match what you typed?

## Environment

Nothing unusual happens during this scenario.
